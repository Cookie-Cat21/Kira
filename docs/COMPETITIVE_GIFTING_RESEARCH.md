# Competitive Gifting Research (Kapruka Benchmarks)

## Objective

Identify proven ecommerce/gifting patterns from companies similar to Kapruka and translate them into actionable product improvements for Kira + Kapruka web experiences.

---

## Companies reviewed

- 1-800-Flowers
- FNP (Ferns N Petals)
- Bloom & Wild
- Floward
- Moonpig
- Goody
- Interflora

---

## What competitors do well

### 1) Occasion reminders that drive repeat purchases

**Seen at:** Moonpig, Bloom & Wild, FNP, Floward

Patterns:
- Save birthdays/anniversaries/holidays and send reminder nudges
- Import from phone/calendar (Moonpig/Bloom & Wild)
- Incentivize setup with offers (Moonpig 40% setup offer, Bloom & Wild credit offer)

Why this matters:
- Turns one-time buyers into repeat buyers
- Improves retention without paid reacquisition every cycle

### 2) Delivery promise clarity before checkout

**Seen at:** 1-800-Flowers, Interflora, FNP

Patterns:
- Same-day cutoff shown clearly (example: order by 3 PM local time)
- Location-specific availability checks
- Delivery windows, expedited surcharge transparency

Why this matters:
- Reduces "can this arrive on time?" anxiety
- Lifts conversion for urgent purchases

### 3) Membership for shipping/service economics

**Seen at:** 1-800-Flowers (Celebrations Passport), Interflora delivery pass

Patterns:
- Annual membership for free standard shipping / reduced service fees
- Cross-brand benefits and member-only offers
- Explicit handling for expedited delivery surcharges

Why this matters:
- Increases order frequency and loyalty
- Improves predictability of repeat purchases

### 4) No-address gifting flow

**Seen at:** Goody, Floward

Patterns:
- Sender initiates gift with recipient contact details only
- Recipient confirms shipping address privately at accept step
- Optional gift swap/choice experiences

Why this matters:
- Removes major checkout blocker ("I don't have their address")
- Enables surprise gifting and higher completion rate

### 5) Guided gift finder

**Seen at:** FNP

Patterns:
- Occasion + recipient + budget + location funnels
- Fast narrowing from broad catalog to short list

Why this matters:
- Faster product discovery
- Lower cognitive load for indecisive shoppers

### 6) Personalization upgrades

**Seen at:** Floward (video card), Moonpig (personalized cards/photos)

Patterns:
- Personalized note/media add-ons
- Messaging templates by occasion

Why this matters:
- Increases emotional value and AOV
- Improves gift "completeness"

### 7) Subscription gifting

**Seen at:** Bloom & Wild, Floward, 1-800-Flowers

Patterns:
- Monthly flowers/gifts
- Easy recurring scheduling

Why this matters:
- Builds recurring revenue
- Improves customer lifetime value

### 8) Business / bulk gifting workflows

**Seen at:** FNP, Goody

Patterns:
- Bulk recipient upload
- Corporate ordering and controlled delivery

Why this matters:
- Opens high-value B2B order streams

## Recommended roadmap for Kapruka

## Priority 1 (highest ROI, lowest platform risk)

1. **Occasion Reminder Engine**
   - Store occasion, recipient, preferred budget, city
   - Trigger reminder journeys (email/SMS/push)
   - One-click "reorder similar" from reminder

2. **Delivery Promise Bar**
   - Show same-day cutoff and eligibility before add-to-cart
   - Expose clear availability by city and selected date
   - Surface any surcharge early

3. **No-Address Gift Link**
   - "Send without address" checkout mode
   - Recipient self-serves address securely after gift acceptance

## Priority 2

4. **Gift Finder Wizard**
   - Occasion -> recipient -> budget -> city -> curated results

5. **Personalized gifting add-ons**
   - Video/photo card
   - Saved message templates
   - Optional premium wrapping

6. **Checkout trust layer**
   - Security, delivery guarantee, policy visibility near payment CTA

## Priority 3

7. **Kapruka Plus membership**
   - Reduced/waived standard delivery fees
   - Member perks and targeted retention offers

8. **Subscription gifting**
   - Weekly/monthly recurring plans for flowers and curated gift bundles

9. **Corporate gifting console**
   - CSV upload recipients
   - Invoice workflow
   - Campaign-level scheduling
